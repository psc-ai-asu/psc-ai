---
title: "Welcome to PSC-AI"
description: "Review the AI agents you use, or see how users rate the agents you build."
---

PSC-AI is a platform for structured feedback on AI agents. It serves two sides:

- **Reviewers** share their experience with AI agents they've used, rating accuracy, helpfulness and task completion.
- **Agent developers** see how their agents perform in production through real user reviews.
