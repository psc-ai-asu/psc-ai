## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to [reviewmyagent.today](https://www.reviewmyagent.today) and choose to create an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Pick a username and enter your email and a password. Your password must:
    - Be at least 8 characters
    - Start with a capital letter
    - End with a special character, such as `!`, `@`, `#`, `$` or `%`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    Check your inbox for a confirmation email and follow the link in it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a reset">
    On the login page, choose to reset your password and enter the email connected to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    You'll receive an email with a password reset code. Enter it to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    Enter a new password that meets the same rules as above.
  </Step>
</Steps>
