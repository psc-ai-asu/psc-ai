## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to [reviewmyagent.today](https://www.reviewmyagent.today) and choose to create an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Pick a username and enter your email and a password that meets the [password requirements](#password-requirements). A checklist under the password field shows which requirements you've met as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    Check your inbox for a confirmation email and follow the link in it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a reset">
    On the login page, choose to reset your password and enter the email connected to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    You'll receive an email with a password reset code. Enter it to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    Enter a new password that meets the [password requirements](#password-requirements).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password requirements

Your password must include:

- At least 12 characters
- A lowercase letter
- An uppercase letter
- A number
- A symbol, such as `!`, `@`, `#`, `$` or `%`

The letters, number and symbol can go anywhere in your password.

<Tip>
  Longer passwords are harder to guess. A short phrase of a few unrelated words, like `Purple canoe 7 pickles!`, is easy to remember and meets every requirement.
</Tip>
