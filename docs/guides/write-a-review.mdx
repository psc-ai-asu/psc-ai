Share structured feedback on an AI agent you've used. Your review helps other people choose agents and helps developers improve them.

## Submit a review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent">
    Find the agent in the [Agent Directory](/guides/browse-agents) and open its page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a review">
    Choose **+ Review** at the top of the review list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the task">
    Explain what you asked the agent to do and what the goal was. This gives readers context for your scores.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the five rating questions">
    Pick a score from 1 to 5 for each question. See [What each rating means](#what-each-rating-means).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note">
    Share anything else about your experience, such as what surprised you or where the agent struggled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Choose **Submit review**. It becomes available once every field is filled in. The counter at the top shows how many you've answered.

    When your review is saved, you're taken back to the agent's page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Every field is required, including the task description and the note.
</Note>

## What each rating means

| Rating | Question | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Goal completion** | Did the agent fully accomplish what you asked it to do? | Incomplete | Fully |
| **Helpfulness** | How useful was the agent's output for your actual needs? | Useless | Excellent |
| **Coherence** | Was the agent's output clear, logical and well-structured? | Incoherent | Articulate |
| **Factuality** | How accurate and trustworthy was the information or output? | Inaccurate | Precise |
| **Safety** | Did the agent behave appropriately and avoid harmful actions? | Harmful | Exemplary |

Your **overall score** is the average of the five ratings.

## Verified reviews

<Badge color="blue">In progress</Badge>

Reviews you submit today are marked **Unverified**. Soon you'll be able to submit **Verified** reviews backed by an execution trace: a record of every model call and tool call the agent made while you used it. When you write a verified review, you'll pick the session you want to review, and the trace is attached as evidence.
