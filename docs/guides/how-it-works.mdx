---

PSC-AI connects two groups of people around the same agents. **Reviewers** use an agent and rate how it did. **Agent developers** onboard their agents and see those ratings. You can be both with one account.

## The flow at a glance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up with a username, email and password. You need an account for every page of the platform except the home page. See [Create an Account](/guides/create-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find an agent">
    Open the **Agent Directory** to see every onboarded agent with its average score. See [Browse Agents](/guides/browse-agents).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review it">
    From an agent's page, choose **+ Review**, describe what you asked it to do and rate it on five questions. See [Write a Review](/guides/write-a-review).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboard your own agents">
    In the **Developer Dashboard**, add your agents so others can find and review them. See [Developer Dashboard](/guides/developer-dashboard).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting around

The bar at the top of every page links to the three main areas:

| Link | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | What the platform does, and the sign-in and sign-up buttons |
| **Agent Directory** | Browsing agents and reading their reviews |
| **Developer Dashboard** | Managing the agents you've onboarded |

## How scores work

Every review rates an agent from 1 to 5 on five questions: goal completion, helpfulness, coherence, factuality and safety. A review's **overall score** is the average of the five. An agent's score in the directory is the average overall score of all its reviews.

Scores are colored the same way everywhere:

| Score | Color |
|---|---|
| 4.0 and above | Green |
| 3.0 to 3.9 | Amber |
| Below 3.0 | Red |

## Unverified and verified reviews

Every review is labeled **Unverified** or **Verified**.

- **Unverified** reviews are based on what the reviewer reports. Every review written today is unverified.
- **Verified** reviews will be backed by an execution trace: a record of what the agent actually did while the reviewer used it.

<Badge color="blue">In progress</Badge> Verified, trace-backed reviews are being built. Until then, every new review is marked Unverified.
